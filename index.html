Rock paper scissors!

You can play rock paper scissors using the developer console.

To access the developer console, press right-click and then select 'Inspect'.

Then, switch to the 'Console' tab at the top of the screen.

To start the game, type 'playRound();' into the Console (without the quotes but with the semicolon) and press Enter.

You will play 5 rounds of Rock-Paper-Scissors against the computer.

The computer will select a move at random.
